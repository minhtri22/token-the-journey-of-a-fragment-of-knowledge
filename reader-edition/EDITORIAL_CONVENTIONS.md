# Editorial source conventions

## Required callout classes

### MINH HỌA — Illustration
For invented numbers, analogies and simplified mental models.

### KẾT QUẢ ĐO — Measured Result
For real experimental values. Must carry an Evidence Note ID.

### RANH GIỚI DIỄN GIẢI — Interpretation Boundary
States what the evidence supports and what it does not support.

These classes must remain distinguishable without relying only on color.

## Scientific invariants

- Do not strengthen claims.
- Preserve PASS / FAIL / UNRESOLVED meaning.
- Preserve exact measured values in Evidence Notes.
- Observation is not mechanism.
- Pattern is not causation.
- Counter zero is not automatically physical zero.
- Do not add speculative successor research.
- Keep Vietnamese-first terminology with English term at first introduction.
